Memberships and training

Fitness options for your goals

These proposed Gym4U services and prices are planning estimates and may change before launch.

Basic Gym Access

Self-guided access to the planned strength and cardio areas. A suitable starting option for members who already have a routine.

PHP 600 / month

Plus Membership

Gym access with one monthly goal review to help members check progress and adjust their workout routine.

PHP 1000 / month

Coached Membership

Gym access plus four one-on-one personal training sessions each month for members seeking structured coaching.

PHP 1500 / month

Personal Training

A proposed single coaching session focused on exercise form, technique, and a practical plan for the member’s goals.

PHP 200 / session
