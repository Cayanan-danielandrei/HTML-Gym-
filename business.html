The Gym4U Business Plan

A proposed community fitness center concept for Magalang, Pampanga, with approachable membership options, coaching, and a welcoming training environment.

Target market Marketing Operations
Planning note: Gym4U is presented as a proposed business. Its descriptions and proposed operating hours are academic planning assumptions, not confirmed operating details.

Target market

The initial market is the Magalang community and nearby areas. These customer groups are planning assumptions; market size and demand should be validated through local surveys before launch.

Students and young adults

People seeking an affordable, convenient place to build an exercise routine alongside school, work, or other commitments.

Local workers and residents

Adults looking for predictable membership pricing and a nearby option for strength and cardio training.

Beginners and returning members

People who may value an orientation, coaching support, or a comfortable environment as they begin or restart training.

Marketing strategy

Promotion and outreach

  • Share workout tips, facility updates, and introductory offers on social media.
  • Plan a launch-week open house with gym tours and beginner equipment orientation.
  • Test a member referral offer and partnerships with nearby schools or local businesses.
  • Use clear service descriptions and proposed prices across print and online promotions.

Pricing and measurement

Use three monthly membership levels so customers can choose between independent gym access and additional coaching. Review inquiries, trial visits, membership sign-ups, and renewals each month to understand which outreach works.

Business operations

Resources and equipment

  • Proposed leased fitness space in Magalang with a reception and changing area.
  • Strength equipment, free weights, cardio machines, lockers, and cleaning supplies.
  • Member sign-in and payment records, posted safety guidance, and first-aid supplies.
  • Planned staff: a manager or attendant, qualified trainers, and cleaning support.

Daily procedures

Proposed hours are Monday to Saturday, 6:00 AM–9:00 PM, subject to lease and demand. Staff would inspect equipment before opening, record member visits, schedule coaching, clean high-touch equipment throughout the day, and complete a closing safety check.

Before launch, the owner would confirm applicable permits, insurance, safety procedures, equipment servicing, and staffing requirements.
